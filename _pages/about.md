I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Belfer Center's Environment and Natural Resources Program and the Science, Technology, and Public Policy Program at the Harvard Kennedy School.

My research focuses on accelerating clean energy innovation and deployment to minimize greenhouse gas emissions and costs while maximizing co-benefits and equity.

I hold a Ph.D. in Science, Technology, and Environmental Policy from Princeton University.
