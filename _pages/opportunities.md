Our lab in the [Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/cee/) at NUS conducts interdisciplinary research on energy and climate policy, combining engineering-based modeling with policy and economic analysis. We focus on clean energy transition and net-zero energy systems, technology innovation, and climate change impacts.

We are recruiting Ph.D. students (starting Spring/Fall 2027), postdocs, and visiting students/scholars (starting 2026) to join the group.



**PhD Students**

Candidates should apply through the Ph.D. program in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the National University of Singapore. Please review the application instructions at https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/programmes/phd-civil-and-environmental-engineeringand mention your interest in working with our group in your application. Full tuition and a competitive stipend will be provided. Students interested in starting in 2027 are strongly encouraged to reach out early to discuss potential research fit and directions.

We are looking for self-motivated individuals who are: 1) strongly interested in interdisciplinary research in energy and climate policy; 2) committed to contributing to an inclusive, respectful, and productive research environment; 3) holders of a bachelor’s or master’s degree in a broadly relevant field (e.g., environmental studies, public policy, economics, business, electrical or industrial engineering, computer science, or mathematics); 4) experienced in relevant research (a plus) and proficient in programming/data analysis (preferred); 5) skilled in scientific writing and communication in English.

Interested candidates are welcome to email Dr. Shangwei Liu at shangweiliu@nus.edu.sg with the subject line “PhD – [Your Full Name] – 2027.” Include: a) your academic CV; b) a brief statement describing your research interests, prior research experience and skills, and career goals.



**Postdocs**

We are looking for postdoctoral researchers to join our lab starting 2026, or at another mutually agreed time.

Current research directions include: 1) energy transition in Singapore and globally, including pathways to net-zero energy systems; 2) technology innovation and energy system modeling; 3) climate change impacts and social cost/damage function estimation.

Qualifications: 1) Ph.D. from a leading university (awarded or expected soon) in a relevant field; 2) strong quantitative skills in modeling and data analysis, with proficiency in at least one programming language (R, Python, or MATLAB); 3) preferred research skills: integrated assessment modeling, energy systems modeling, industrial ecology methods, techno-economic analysis, econometrics, optimization, statistical analysis.

Interested candidates are welcome to email Dr. Shangwei Liu at shangweiliu@nus.edu.sg with the subject line “Postdoc – [Your Full Name] – 2026.” Include: a) your academic CV; b) a brief statement describing your research interests, prior research experience and skills, and career goals; c) one first-author paper.



**Visiting Students/Scholars**

Qualifications: Current PhD students in relevant fields or scholars with aligned research interests. Self-funded visitors are welcome. If you require support, please state your funding needs and potential sources in your materials.

Interested candidates are welcome to email Dr. Shangwei Liu at shangweiliu@nus.edu.sg with the subject line “Visiting Students/Scholars – [Your Full Name] – 2026.” Include: a) your academic CV; b) a brief statement describing your research interests and plans during the visit.
