<div class="card card--row" markdown="1">
### Ph.D. Students

We are recruiting **one Ph.D. student** to start in **Fall 2027**, working on energy and power system modeling, nuclear power, and climate policy.

**Requirements**

- Strong interest in interdisciplinary research, with the ability to work independently and in a team, and a high degree of self-motivation.
- Applicants from a broad range of backgrounds are welcome, including but not limited to engineering, environmental science, and computer science. See the official NUS application website for degree requirements.
- Relevant research experience is preferred.
- Strong English communication and academic writing skills. An IELTS score of 7.0 or above is required.
- Formal applications must be submitted through the official NUS admissions system.

If you are interested, feel free to reach out early with your CV.

- [NUS Graduate School Admissions](https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/admissions/)
- [NUS Civil and Environmental Engineering Graduate Programmes](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/cee/graduate/)
- [NUS Graduate School PhD Scholarships](https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/)
</div>
