Our lab in the [Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/cee/) at NUS conducts interdisciplinary research on energy and climate policy, combining engineering-based modeling with policy and economic analysis. We focus on clean energy transition and net-zero energy systems, technology innovation, and climate change impacts.

**Ph.D. Students**

Ph.D. positions are available starting Fall 2027. We are looking for motivated students with strong quantitative backgrounds and interest in energy systems, climate policy, or related areas.

If you are interested, please feel free to reach out early — email Dr. Shangwei Liu at shangweiliu@nus.edu.sg with your CV and a brief note about your research interests.
